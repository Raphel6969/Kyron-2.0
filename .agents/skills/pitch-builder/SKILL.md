# EXECUTION PROTOCOL

1. **Lock Down the 90-Second Judge Click-Path in `DEMO.md`:**
   - **Step 1 (0:00–0:20):** Land on public production URL. Hook the judge with immediate live status.
   - **Step 2 (0:20–0:50):** Trigger the differentiator with a single click. Show sub-10ms performance or state change.
   - **Step 3 (0:50–1:15):** Click `/impact`. Show dynamic arithmetic calculated from seeded database records.
   - **Step 4 (1:15–1:30):** Point to audit proof or cryptographic trail. Wrap with production readiness.
   - Verify zero terminal commands, zero Postman, and zero `localhost` references.

2. **Draft the Pitch Narrative in `docs/pitch/outline.md`:**
   - **The Problem Hook (30s):** The status quo loss or disparity (citing `docs/IMPACT.md` baseline).
   - **The Technical Moat (60s):** Why simple wrappers fail and how our architecture solves it.
   - **The Live Demo (90s):** Executing the exact path from `DEMO.md`.
   - **Unit Economics & Road Ahead (30s):** Naming Honest Limits and stating next commercial milestones.

3. **Formulate Q&A Defense Matrix:**
   Answer the 3 hardest questions generated by `judge-sim`. Provide direct, non-defensive technical answers backed by codebase evidence.

4. **Enforce 60-Line Cap:**
   Ensure `DEMO.md` does not exceed 60 lines.
